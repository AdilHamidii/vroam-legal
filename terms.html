Prices are shown in US dollars or euros, depending on your device's region, and you are charged in the currency shown at checkout. Payment is processed by Stripe, with Apple Pay or a card; we never see or store your card details. By purchasing a plan, you authorize the charge for the amount shown at checkout.

Store credit, gift codes and promotions. Store credit (for example from referrals, loyalty rewards or a redeemed gift code) is applied at checkout in the currency it was issued in. It has no cash value, cannot be withdrawn as cash, and always leaves a small minimum amount to be paid by card. A gift code is single-use and worth the amount paid for it. Free trial. Where offered, a free trial eSIM is available in selected countries, once per account, and requires you to be signed in (not a guest). 5. Delivery of eSIMs

Once payment succeeds, your eSIM is provisioned by eSIM Access and made available in the app for installation. Provisioning is not always instantaneous, and delivery times can vary depending on the provider.

An eSIM you install on your device keeps working even if you later delete your Sielli eSIM account — account deletion removes your data from our backend, not a profile already installed on your device.

6. Refunds

eSIM data plans are prepaid mobile data services supplied by local mobile network operators and activated on your device. Provisioning delivers the activation credential; the service itself is provided by the operator's network in your destination.

  • Unused plans: you can request a refund in the app (Account → Order history → Request a refund) within 14 days of purchase. If the plan has not been used (no data consumed), the refund is approved automatically. The refunded eSIM may be deactivated.
  • Plans that were never successfully provisioned are refunded in full automatically.
  • Plans with data already used, and other cases (including a gift code that has already been redeemed) are reviewed by a person; we aim to reply within 2 business days. Email adil.hamidii123@gmail.com for anything outside the in-app window — we will review it case by case.

A refund returns the amount you paid by card to your original payment method (it usually appears within 5–10 days, depending on your bank). Any store credit you spent on the order is returned as store credit, not cash, and rewards earned on a refunded order are reversed. An unredeemed gift code that is refunded is cancelled.
