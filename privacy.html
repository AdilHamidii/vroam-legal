Sielli eSIM (formerly Zeli eSIM) — Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 29, 2026

1. Who we are

Sielli eSIM is an iOS app for buying travel eSIM data plans, available on the App Store.

Sielli eSIM is operated by AntherSystems, a sole proprietorship of Adil Hamidi, registered at 6 rue Marechal Leclerc, B415, 21000 Dijon, France.

For any question about this policy or your data, contact us at adil.hamidii123@gmail.com.

2. What data we collect and why

We collect only what is needed to run the app:

  • Email address — collected when you sign in with Sign in with Apple, used to identify your account and contact you about your orders.
  • User identifier — the unique account identifier created at sign-in, used to link your purchases and eSIMs to your account.
  • Purchase history — the eSIM data plans you buy, used to show your orders and installed eSIMs in the app.

All of this data is linked to your account and used only for app functionality — to let you sign in, buy a plan, and see your eSIMs. We do not use it for advertising, we do not run analytics SDKs, we do not track you across apps or websites, and we do not sell your data.

Sign-in

Sielli eSIM supports Sign in with Apple only. We never see or store your Apple password, and depending on your choice, Apple may share only a private relay email address with us.

On-device storage

The app keeps a small local record on your device (in Apple's UserDefaults) of which eSIMs you've already installed, so the app can show the right install status. This record stays on your device and is not sent to our servers.

3. Payments

Payments are processed by Stripe (Stripe Payments Europe). Sielli eSIM never sees, collects, or stores your card number or other card details — that data is handled entirely by Stripe. Prices are shown in USD.

4. eSIM delivery

Once you buy a plan, your eSIM is provisioned by our provider, eSIM Access. Your eSIM profile is installed on your device and keeps working there even if you later delete your Sielli eSIM account.

5. Where your data is stored

Our backend runs on Supabase, hosted in the eu-central-1 (Frankfurt, EU) region. Your account data, order history, and eSIM records are stored there, within the EU.

7. Data retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account exists. If you delete your account, we delete your personal data from our backend (see below). Stripe and eSIM Access retain their own financial and provider records for as long as required by their own legal and accounting obligations — this is outside our control and is normal for any regulated payment or telecom provider.

8. Account deletion

You can delete your account at any time from Settings → account deletion in the app. This deletes your account and associated personal data from our backend. Notes:

  • Stripe and eSIM Access retain financial/provider records as required by law.
  • Any eSIM you already installed keeps working on your device — deleting your account does not disable a provisioned eSIM.
